A Letter For You

I have started this letter a hundred times in my head. Nothing ever feels like enough. But here it is anyway. Imperfect. Honest. Real.

You walked into my life like a song I did not know I needed to hear. And now I cannot imagine silence. You are the first thought in my morning and the last one before I sleep. Not because I try. Because you just stayed there. In my mind. In my heart. In places I did not even know were empty before you.

You make me want to be better. Not louder. Not bigger. Better. Softer where I was hard. Open where I was closed. You did that without asking. Just by being you.

I do not know what the future holds. Nobody does. But I know I want you in mine. Not in the background. Not as a maybe. Right in the center. Right where you belong.

So here I am. No games. No hiding behind screens. Just me telling you that you mean more to me than any word could hold.
